About INTERNSHIPAI

INTERNSHIPAI is a smart career advisor built for students who want clarity on their internship readiness. By combining academic details, current skills, and target internship requirements, it generates a personalized report covering eligibility, skill match, a learning roadmap and project recommendations to guide the next steps in your career journey.

  • Fast, structured career analysis
  • Actionable 4-week learning roadmap
  • Practical project recommendations
